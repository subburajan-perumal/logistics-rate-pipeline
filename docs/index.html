Why it plateaus at 1.82× and not the 4× the plan assumed: the pool cannot beat the critical path. One feed (aurora) serves two sequential cursor pages at ~1.9 s each, so no worker count finishes under ≈ 3.8 s while the other seven feeds sum to ≈ 2.9 s. The number is committed as measured (decision D-40); the only fix is page-level parallelism, which cursor pagination forbids by contract.
